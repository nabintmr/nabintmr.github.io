About Nabin

Nabin Tamrakar provides financial planning guidance and community leadership across Eastern Nepal. As a licensed agent with Nepal Life Insurance Company, he works directly with families and professionals to build disciplined life coverage, retirement pensions, and education funds tailored to realistic household budgets.

Alongside his professional practice, Nabin serves as Area Coordinator for GAT Area II in District 325 C of Lions Club International. His work bridges accessible community welfare — organizing regular blood donation drives, health screenings, and grassroots relief — with pragmatic financial literacy in personal insurance and capital markets.
